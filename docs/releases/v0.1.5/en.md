## Features

- **Intel Macs** — Install VigilShell on Intel Macs with a dedicated macOS installer.
- **Architecture-aware updates** — Keep in-app updates on the installed architecture, including Intel apps running under Rosetta.

## Upgrade Notes

- For a manual installation, choose the Apple Silicon or Intel installer for your Mac.
- Users on 0.1.3 or later can update in the app; users on earlier published versions must download the DMG and replace the app manually.
